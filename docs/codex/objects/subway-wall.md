# Subway wall

**Status:** Built.

The tiled wall in the subway where you spray your name, once, and it stays for good. Nobody can paint over it. The wall shows the newest tags. The wall is the visitor book: the book on a stand that listed every tag was removed after the UI playtest of 2026-09-29.
