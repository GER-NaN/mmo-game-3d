# Street lights

**Status:** Built.

The first repair job. The AI takes out the street lights on Main Street; you see it only at night, the dark street next to the lit one. A player takes the job in Town repairs and repairs the junction box, and the lights come on for everyone. A repair holds for a while, then the AI takes them out again.
