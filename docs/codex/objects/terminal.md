# Terminal

**Status:** Built.

A modern-day computer out in the world: library terminals, your phone, your laptop, the gaming rig in your bedroom, the supercomputer at your school. You walk up to one and Go Online, and a separate screen opens with its own look: the Terminal World.

Every safe zone has several free standard terminals. Specific terminals look the same but are bound to a place or a target, such as a data centre terminal where you must hack the control panel app or seed a virus. One player per terminal. In the AI-controlled towns terminals are buggy or shut off. Old Town has a library terminal and a street kiosk.
