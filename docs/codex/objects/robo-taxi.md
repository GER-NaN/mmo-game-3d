# Robo taxi

**Status:** Built.

A driverless taxi. Call one at the taxi stand and ride it: you sit in the cabin while the car drives its route. The AI puts rootkits in the taxis, and a taxi with one is not used until someone cleans it. Robo taxi fares are one of the ways money leaves the world.
