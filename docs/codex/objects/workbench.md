# Workbench

**Status:** Built.

Where items are repaired and built. Today: take the dead battery out of a phone and put a full one in. It has an item slot, a component slot and an apply button.

Workbenches are easy to reach, one or two per town in public, and they do not look the same: a picnic table in the park, a reading corner in the library, a shop, a desk in your room. The electronics shop has one.
