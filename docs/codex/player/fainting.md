# Fainting

**Status:** Built. The loss of items is not.

There is no player death. At 0 HP you faint, and helpful strangers carry you "magically" back to the nearest town.

By design fainting has a real cost: whatever hurt you can damage or steal from your inventory or equipment, scaled by tier and never total (common items often, high tier rarely), and waking far from where you were costs the walk back. Whether the nearest town includes the small towns in the wild, or a camp, is open.
