# HP

**Status:** Built.

The player's health meter, 100 to 0. Below 50 you move slower; at 0 you faint. HP does not grow with progression. It comes back slowly when nothing has hurt you for a while.

Drones and AI-controlled things do the harm. Armor, to protect a player without raising HP, is an idea.
