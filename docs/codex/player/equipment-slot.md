# Equipment slot

**Status:** Built.

A slot for an item you can use: device, tool and drone. All slots show from the start and stay empty until you fill them, by dragging an item from the inventory. A new player fills one, with their phone.

Items can have slots of their own: the phone has one for its battery, and a drone will have its components.
