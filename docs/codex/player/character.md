# Character

**Status:** Built.

Your body in the world. At the start you choose a name and a look. Display names are not unique and cannot be changed. An account has two characters, and more can be bought.

The look should become advanced: the full spectrum for head, body and hair colour, and clothing and flair (accessories, sunglasses, hats, vests, jewellery, backpacks, drone colours). Today there is a character creator, character select with two slots, and a wardrobe.
