# Your rig

**Status:** Decided, not built.

Part of the game is "your rig": phone, laptop, batteries, exoskeleton, personal drones, monitoring rig. The device is one equippable line among dozens that each level up: defence drones, the EMP gun, the virus catalogue, the GPU tier. The AI can damage these electronically and hack them when it gets near you: a temporary scramble, damage that needs repair, or a complete brick.
