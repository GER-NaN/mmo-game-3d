# Weaponised technology

**Status:** Decided, not built.

The AI infiltrates and weaponises the technology near you: hacked billboards blind you, an AI-driven Tesla rams you, a drone attacks, an RC car, a humanoid robot, a connected vehicle made to explode, escalators that make you fall, the TV in the shop window that explodes. This is environmental danger, not a battle system, and there is no turn-based combat.
