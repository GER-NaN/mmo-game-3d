# Firewall

**Status:** Decided, not built.

What stands between the AI and a town's systems. The AI can randomly break through firewalls and kill part of the electric grid. Players "restore the firewall" from the terminal to protect a power station or a sewer plant, and an attack can reach you as a push notification: "Agent Defense Required: AI infiltrated local firewall, log in to defend".
