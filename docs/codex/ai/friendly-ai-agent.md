# Friendly AI agent

**Status:** Decided, not built.

Friendly, controlled AI agents exist and work on the player's behalf. They are an endgame item: they fight in the terminal world and protect you in the physical one. "Back on my gaming rig I have three Defender Agents watching my moves; they will protect me as I run through the unfriendly zone."
