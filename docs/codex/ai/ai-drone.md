# AI drone

**Status:** Built. Surveillance drones fly over Old Town.

The AI's drones. Today they are surveillance drones that circle over Old Town: the AI's eyes are dense in towns. An EMP knocks one out of the sky, and the Town cameras app lets a player spot and report them for pay.

By design the AI infiltrates and weaponises drones: one attacks with anything it has (drops a dollhouse on your head, spills water on you). In the generated wild, rogue drones roam the forest, and salvage from broken drones is a source of parts.
