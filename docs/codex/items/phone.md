# Phone

**Status:** Built.

A door into the Terminal World you carry. It is the only device that does not take the whole screen: the terminal shows centred, like a phone held in front of your face, with the world still going on behind you, and you cannot move while on it. It has a simpler layout than a laptop or a gaming rig.

It has one slot, for a battery; its charge is its battery's charge, and a dead phone cannot go online. A new player arrives with a starter phone at ten percent. Others see you with the phone up, head down, the screen glowing.
