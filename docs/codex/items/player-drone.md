# Player drone

**Status:** Decided, not built. The drone slot shows, empty.

Drones are the heroes of Guild Wars: companions with specialties. Roles have real trade-offs: pure defence, pure attack or anti-drone, mixed support, so composition is a choice. Observation drones give line of sight or better GPS. Autonomous protection drones defend your body while you are online, a mid-game feature. A drone has batteries and components (spy camera, EMP pulse generator).
