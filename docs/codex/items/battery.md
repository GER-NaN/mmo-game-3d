# Battery

**Status:** Built.

Fits a phone. It drains from 100 to 0: about three days when only carried, about three hours while online on it. At zero the phone is dead and you lose your terminal connection. A dead battery is swapped at a workbench for a full one, from the ground or the shop ($8). Batteries stack when full; one that is not full is its own item.
