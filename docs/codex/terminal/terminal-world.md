# Terminal World

**Status:** Built.

The online world. It presents as a custom OS with its own state, and devices (phone, laptop, stationary terminal) are doors into it. Every device except the phone takes the whole screen. It is cold in look, where the physical world is warm, and it must still feel like an extension of it.

Everything behind a terminal is where the player sees the whole game: locked apps show with a notice. Your body stays in the physical world, vulnerable, while you are online.
