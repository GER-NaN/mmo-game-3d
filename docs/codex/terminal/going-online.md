# Going online

**Status:** Built.

Being at a terminal is a state the world shows; the verbs are "Go Online" and "Go Offline". Online, your body cannot run unless you unplug. Unplugging is one click, and the cost is what you leave behind: mid-hack you probably lose the hack, or the GPU you had in there. Party members do not unplug you; they fight the drones off with their tools.
