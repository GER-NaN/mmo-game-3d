# Townsperson

**Status:** Built.

Someone who lives in town and walks a loop round a block, stopping now and then. Talk to one and you get a line of small talk: they know about the AI from the news and blow it off, and they do not see the fight. In Old Town: Wren (out walking), Old Tomas, and Ines, a driver.
