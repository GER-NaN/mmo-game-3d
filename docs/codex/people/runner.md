# Runner

**Status:** Decided, not built.

A high-level player who runs a low-level one to an advanced part of the game, and gets paid for it, as in Guild Wars. It works because the whole party changes zones together. A Runner's protection scales with their progression, so their passengers pass largely unmolested. The author sees Runner as a natural emergent profession.
