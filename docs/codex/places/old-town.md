# Old Town

**Status:** Built.

The first town, and the hub. It is hand-built, and the rings of the wild spread out from it.

Built: Main Street with its street lights and junction box, a library terminal and a street kiosk, the taxi stand and its robo taxis, a recycler, security cameras, townspeople walking their loops and drones overhead. Doors lead to the outskirts, the meadows, the electronics shop, the college and the subway. Its local chat is "Old Town Chat".
