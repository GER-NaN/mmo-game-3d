# Training Grounds

**Status:** Decided, not built. It is built last.

The game's tutorial island, in the manner of the original Guild Wars starting zone. On the very first load you start there and see no other part of the world. You cannot return.

It forces you through nearly every mechanic: quests, battle, crafting, trading, selling, making a team, drones as heroes, and a stripped-down terminal. Its loop is exaggerated and easy: collect parts from broken drones to build a GPU, put it into a broken terminal for your first time online, repair the grid, trade a GPU for a defence drone, fight a boss drone with another player.

To get out you fight an impossible boss, faint, lose everything and wake in a town. The phone is your reward for leaving. You can skip it by starting the exit mission right away. Skill levels earned there are kept.
