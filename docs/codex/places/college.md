# College

**Status:** Built. One college, a building in Old Town.

Where careers start. The first time, a player takes the Class there; after that they talk to the registrar to change career, and they go back to their original college to meet their old professor for a rank-up.

Colleges can be anywhere, probably each its own zone, several spread through the world. The one built is in Old Town, with Mara the registrar and Professor Okafor.
