# Data centre

**Status:** Decided, not built.

The top of the equipment, and the endgame weapon against the AI. A group builds it, in the EVE manner of a player-made mega-structure; only a powerful group can afford one. It costs consumables and real time, about a week.

Where it stands matters: inside the AI's coverage, the AI acts against it going up; somewhere remote, it is safe. For its owners it is a safe zone in the wild, a repair centre, a currency boost (mine crypto, GPU packs), a source of compute against the AI, and a major influence in the fight for control.

It decays and needs so much upkeep that materials are shipped in by airdrop, rail or drone. It can be raided, and when one falls the AI gains something. A data centre raid: hack the central system from the control room, fight the security drones, cut the power, then reboot the building to secure it.
