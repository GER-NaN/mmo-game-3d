# Electronics shop

**Status:** Built.

The shop in Old Town, an interior zone of its own: big enough to walk a few aisles of cosmetic scenery, with posters that foreshadow drones. Dee, the shopkeeper, sells a battery for $8, and a few things a new player cannot afford yet: an EMP Emitter ($10), an Enhanced RAM stick ($25), an Advanced GPU core ($60) and a phone ($120). It also has a workbench.
