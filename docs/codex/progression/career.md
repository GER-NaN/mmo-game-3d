# Career

**Status:** Built.

A specialization: broad, using many skills, and one at a time. Your best skills point you to one. A career has a gate (a minimum level in its supporting skills), its own experience (from those skills and from using its own abilities) and five ranks. You start one at a college after the Class; changing career at the registrar is permanent and loses all progress in the old one, but skills stay. A career is optional, and your career and rank are public.

Built: Mechanical Engineer and Computer Scientist. Named: Drone Operator, Electrical Engineer, Librarian, Truck Driver, Artist. The list is open.
