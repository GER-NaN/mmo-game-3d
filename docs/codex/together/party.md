# Party

**Status:** Built.

A temporary group that does things together: a data centre raid, a substation repair, a coop hack. Invite by clicking another player, accept or deny, leave back to solo. A party holds until someone leaves; distance and disconnects do not break it. The whole party goes through a door together. Terminal parties form in the online meeting room.
