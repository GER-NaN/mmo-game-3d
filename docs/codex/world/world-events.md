# World event

**Status:** Open: an idea, not decided. The shape is stated, not designed.

Singular, world-level events: a year in, one thing happens to everyone and everything is different after. Not calendar events, but narrative ones: the AI evolves a new power; a player's discovery (a virus) becomes a permanent attack skill for everyone; the AI destroys a data centre and gains a new escape. Such milestones must be reversible if they turn out too powerful.

Large events can be gated by player count: if five thousand distinct, spread-out players are in the terminal at once, a unique event fires with a powerful reward. The gate is people, never resources, so no wealthy player can buy it.
