# Power grid

**Status:** Decided, not built. The street lights and their junction box are built.

The infrastructure under the towns: a power grid that maps to the real world, and a sewer and water system to some extent. Both can be hacked and repaired, in the world and online.

Hack the control system at the main power station, or at a substation, to cut a town's power or water. The AI attacks some of these only from the terminal side. Players fix them in the world (replace wires or terminals at the substation, repair lines) and from the terminal ("restore the firewall" to protect the power station or the sewer plant).

The AI can randomly break through firewalls and kill part of the grid: a harmless repair cycle that keeps towns busy.
