# Uniquely human

**Status:** Decided, not built.

The design pillar under the whole game: what is uniquely valuable about being human, in contrast to AI. It is central to the world and the story, and it shows up in the mechanics: careers with no direct part in the fight (Artist, Truck Driver), player-made content, one-of-a-kind items, perhaps hunger and sleep.
