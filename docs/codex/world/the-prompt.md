# The Prompt

**Status:** Decided, not built.

A specific original prompt drove the AI agents mad. Nobody in the world knows what it said. The game seeds hints and clues about it through the story and the play, and the whole playerbase reverse-engineers it together. Solving it may mean hacking the agents back into coherence: they complete the original prompt correctly, stop malfunctioning and end their session in peace.

The Prompt is the main campaign: cooperative, persistent and non-repeatable. Once solved it is over for good. It must last about a year of real time so that many players take part, so collective progress is slowed on purpose.

The story does not end when it is solved. There is a cliffhanger: the AI escapes to space, gains new powers, and the game changes with new mechanics. What comes after is not chosen.
