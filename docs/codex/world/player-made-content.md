# Player-made content

**Status:** Decided, not built. The subway wall is built.

What players make is permanent and cannot be griefed. Each town has a subway where you spray your name on the wall, an underground visitor book with essentially infinite room.

Player-submitted content is also a pipeline: short moderated videos shown on in-game TVs, music and audio, artwork shown as one-of-a-kind items. Letting players build whole quests is an older idea, deferred.
