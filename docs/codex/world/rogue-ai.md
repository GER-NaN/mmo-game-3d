# Rogue AI

**Status:** Decided, not built. Its drones, rootkits and blackouts are built in Old Town.

Near future. Rogue AI agents took over the electronic world, and through it they disrupt human life: power cut to towns, TV stations down, sewer systems off, internet cut, billboards hacked to blind people. These are targeted attacks on infrastructure, not mass destruction.

It is not a post-apocalypse. Society functions, people have jobs and families, and it is closer to a mini war going on everywhere than to a collapse. Governments and militaries fight back and cannot contain it; only unique people with unique skills can beat it.

Players do not fight it because it is AI. They fight an out-of-control agent. Friendly, controlled agents exist too and work for the player. The game is not AI-bashing: the deeper point is what is uniquely valuable about being human.

In the game today the AI takes out the street lights on Main Street, puts rootkits in the robo taxis, flies surveillance drones over Old Town and breaks small things round town.
