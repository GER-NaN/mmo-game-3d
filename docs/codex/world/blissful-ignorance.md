# Blissful ignorance

**Status:** Decided, not built. Townspeople's small talk is built.

People know about the AI and blow it off. They hear "AI did this" on the news and treat it as normal. The people in town do not see the drone battles in front of them: they know about things, but not about the fight. One of the core ideas and lines of the game. An endgame mission can be to hijack the news feed and broadcast the truth.
