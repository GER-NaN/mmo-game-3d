# Real-time travel

**Status:** Decided, not built.

The map follows real geography, and travel takes about its real time: a flight as long as a real flight, Boston to New York by train about ninety minutes, Chicago to Philadelphia on foot about fifty-seven days. It is a feature: take the train, close the game, and your character arrives anyway. Long travel comes with a reward, such as landing somewhere hard to reach.
